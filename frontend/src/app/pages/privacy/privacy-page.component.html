Privacy

Board Vault is a free side project. This page explains in plain words what it keeps about you, why, who helps run it, and how to ask about it.

Who is responsible

Board Vault is made by Kois Studio. Kois Studio is not a company: the person responsible for your data is {{ controllerName }}, in Spain. For anything about your data, write to {{ contactEmail }}.

What Board Vault keeps, and why

  • Your account: your email, username, display name, avatar and language. Clerk handles your password; Board Vault never sees it.
  • What you add: your games, wishlist and ratings, and what your groups record about their game nights.
  • Usage statistics: which pages you open and which features you use, under a pseudonym instead of your name, never what you write. You can turn them off in Settings → Privacy.
  • Technical records: for each request, the server notes the page or route asked for, whether it worked, and your account number. Vercel keeps these for about an hour. When you are not signed in, your IP address is used to limit repeated requests and is forgotten within minutes.
  • Your emails to us: stored with our email provider in Switzerland, and kept while they are useful to answer you and follow up.

To have an account you need a username and a way to sign in; without them, Board Vault cannot give you one. Everything else is your choice.

Your account and what you add are needed to give you the service you sign up for (GDPR, Article 6(1)(b)). Usage statistics, technical records, your emails to us and the history groups keep rest on legitimate interests: keeping Board Vault working, safe and getting better, answering you, and letting groups keep their shared history (Article 6(1)(f)).

Board Vault shows no ads, sells no data and does not track you on other sites.

What other people see

  • Members of your groups see your display name, username and avatar, the games on your shelf, the game nights you take part in, and what you change in the group's history.
  • Your email, wishlist, prices and purchase dates stay private from other people. Your ratings are not shown with your name; they count towards a game's average rating in your groups and across Board Vault.
  • The person responsible can see accounts and game proposals to run the service. The admin pages show usage statistics only as totals.

People without an account

A group can include people who have no account yet. Group members can record their name, the games they own or like, and the game nights they come to, so the group keeps its shared history. To invite one of them to take over that history, the group owner can enter their email: it is used only for that invitation and kept for 30 days at most.

If you are one of these people and want to see, correct or remove what was recorded about you, ask the group owner or write to {{ contactEmail }}.

Who helps run Board Vault

These companies process data for Board Vault to provide their service. You can ask us for details of the safeguards for transfers outside the EU.

  • Clerk (Clerk, Inc., United States): accounts and sign-in, and the emails with sign-in codes and invitations. It may process data outside the EU, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.
  • Vercel (Vercel Inc., United States): runs the website and the server. The server runs in Dublin, Ireland. Transfers to the United States follow the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Turso (ChiselStrike Inc., United States): the database, stored in Ireland.
  • Upstash (Upstash, Inc., United States): short-lived copies of data that make pages load faster, stored in Frankfurt, Germany, and deleted within a day. It follows the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long it is kept

  • Your account stays until you delete it in Settings → Account. Deleting it ends your sign-in at once and erases your email, username, name, avatar, shelf, wishlist and ratings. The game nights you took part in stay in your groups' history, with you shown as “Deleted account”.
  • A group's data stays as long as the group. Deleting a group deletes its game nights and its history.
  • Usage statistics are deleted after 30 days. Only daily counts with no account attached are kept.
  • Technical records last about an hour, and the short-lived copies up to a day.
  • Backups: before a change to the database, a copy is taken and kept privately, only while it may be needed to undo a mistake. The database company also keeps its own short-term backups.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, get a copy, correct it, delete it or limit how it is used. You can do most of it yourself: change your profile in Settings, turn off usage statistics in Settings → Privacy, and delete your account in Settings → Account.

You can also object to the uses that rest on legitimate interests: tell us why, and we will stop unless there is a stronger reason to continue.

For anything else, write to {{ contactEmail }}. Requests about your rights get an answer within a month. If you think your data is handled wrongly, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD) or to the one where you live.

Cookies and your browser

Board Vault stores only what sign-in and the preferences you choose need, so there is no cookie banner:

  • Clerk's sign-in cookies, to keep you signed in.
  • A cookie named bv_lang that remembers your language for a year.
  • In your browser's storage: your theme, colours and text size, and whether the language follows your account.

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Security

Connections are encrypted. The server checks every request against what you are allowed to see. Board Vault's code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check how it works.
