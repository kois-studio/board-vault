Privacy

Board Vault is a free side project. This page explains in plain words what it keeps about you, why, who helps run it, and how to ask about it.

Who is responsible

Board Vault is made by Kois Studio. Kois Studio is not a company: the person responsible for your data is {{ controllerName }}, in Spain. For anything about your data, write to {{ contactEmail }}. A person reads and answers every message.

What Board Vault keeps, and why

  • Your account: your email, username, display name, avatar and language. Clerk handles your password; Board Vault never sees it.
  • What you add: your games, wishlist, ratings and reviews, and what your groups record about their game nights.
  • Usage statistics: which pages you open and which features you use, under a pseudonym instead of your name, never what you write. You can turn them off in Settings → Privacy.
  • Technical records: for each request, the server notes the address asked for, the answer and your account number. Vercel keeps these for about an hour. Your IP address is used only to stop abuse and is forgotten within minutes.
  • Your emails to us: kept while they are useful to answer you and follow up.

Most of this is needed to give you the service you sign up for (GDPR, Article 6(1)(b)). Usage statistics and technical records rest on our legitimate interest in keeping Board Vault working, safe and getting better (Article 6(1)(f)), and you can object to them at any time.

Board Vault shows no ads, sells no data, does not track you on other sites and builds no profiles of you.

What other people see

  • Members of your groups see your display name, username and avatar, the games on your shelf, the game nights you take part in, and what you change in the group's history.
  • Your email, wishlist, prices and purchase dates stay private. Your ratings count towards your groups' average rating of a game.
  • The person responsible can see accounts and game proposals to run the service, and usage statistics only as totals.

People without an account

A group can include people who have no account yet. Members can record their name, the games they own or like, and the game nights they come to. To invite one of them to take over that history, the group owner can enter their email: it is used only for that invitation and kept for 30 days at most.

If you are one of these people and want to see, correct or remove what was recorded about you, ask the group owner or write to {{ contactEmail }}.

Who helps run Board Vault

These companies process data for Board Vault, only to provide their service:

  • Clerk (Clerk, Inc., United States): accounts and sign-in, and the emails with sign-in codes and invitations. It may process data outside the EU, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.
  • Vercel (Vercel Inc., United States): runs the website and the server. The server runs in Dublin, Ireland. Transfers to the United States follow the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Turso (ChiselStrike Inc., United States): the database, stored in Ireland.
  • Upstash (Upstash, Inc., United States): short-lived copies of data that make pages load faster, stored in Frankfurt, Germany, and deleted within a day. It follows the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long it is kept

  • Your account stays until you delete it in Settings → Account. Deleting it is immediate and permanent. Game nights you took part in still count you, shown as “Deleted account”, because they belong to the group too.
  • A group's data stays as long as the group. Deleting a group deletes its game nights and its history.
  • Usage statistics are deleted after 30 days. Only daily totals that belong to nobody are kept.
  • Technical records last about an hour, and the short-lived copies up to a day.
  • Backups: before a change to the database, a copy is taken and kept privately, only while it may be needed to undo a mistake. The database company also keeps its own short-term backups.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your data, to limit how it is used, or object to it. You can do most of it yourself: change your profile in Settings, turn off usage statistics in Settings → Privacy, and delete your account in Settings → Account.

For anything else, write to {{ contactEmail }} and you will get an answer within a month. If you think your data is handled wrongly, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD) or to the one where you live.

Cookies and your browser

Board Vault stores only what it needs to work, so there is no cookie banner:

  • Clerk's sign-in cookies, to keep you signed in.
  • A cookie named bv_lang that remembers your language for a year.
  • In your browser's storage: your theme, colours and text size, and whether the language follows your account.

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Passwords are handled by Clerk, never by Board Vault. The server checks every request against what you are allowed to see. Board Vault's code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check how it works.
