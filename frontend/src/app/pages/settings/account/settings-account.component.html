Delete account

This can’t be undone. Deleting your account:

  • Removes your shelf, wishlist, ratings, notifications and invitations.
  • Takes you out of your groups and their upcoming game nights.
  • Keeps the nights you already played, so your groups’ history stays complete. You appear there as “Deleted account”, without your name or avatar.
  • Passes each group you own to the member who joined it first. A group with nobody else in it is deleted.
  • Removes your sign-in, so you can’t sign back in to this account.
