Privacy boundaries

Your private shelf, wishlist, and account settings are yours. Group members see only the shared group context needed for decisions, sessions, and group memory.

A group owner can manage group membership and group decisions; that does not expose your private shelf as a public catalogue.

Delete account

Self-service deletion is not enabled yet. We are defining the retention and group-history policy before adding an irreversible account action.

Until then, use Clerk to manage your sign-in methods. If you need the account removed during the private beta, contact the project owner so the request can be reviewed safely.
