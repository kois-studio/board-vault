# Code of Conduct

We want Board Vault contributions to be welcoming, constructive, and safe.
Participants must communicate respectfully, assume good faith while resolving
technical disagreements, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats,
sexualized conduct, doxxing, or publication of another person's private
information.

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when conduct violates these expectations. Report a concern
privately through the repository's security/contact channel rather than a
public issue when it involves personal or safety-sensitive information.
