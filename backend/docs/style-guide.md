# Style guide

## Background

☀️ white mode:
  - main: `white` #ffffff
  - secondary: `bg-gray-50` #f9fafb
  - tertiary: `bg-gray-100` #f3f4f6

🌑 dark mode:
  - main: `bg-zinc-900` #18181b
  - secondary: `bg-zinc-800` #27272a
  - tertiary: `bg-zinc-700` #3f3f46

## Border

☀️ white mode:
  - main: `border-gray-100` #f3f4f6
  - strong: `border-gray-200` #e5e7eb

🌑 dark mode:
  - main: `border-gray-800` #1f2937
  - strong: `border-gray-700` #374151

## Text

☀️ white mode:
  - disabled: `text-gray-300` #d1d5db
  - subtle: `text-gray-400` #9ca3af
  - normal: `text-gray-500` #6b7280
  - muted: `text-gray-600` #4b5563
  - strong: `text-gray-700` #374151

## Accent

success:
  - background: `bg-emerald-100` #d1fae5
  - content: `text-emerald-500` #10b981

alert:
  - background: `bg-orange-100` #ffedd5
  - content: `text-orange-500` #f97316

error:
  - background: `bg-red-100` #ffe4e6
  - content: `text-red-500` #f43f5e
  
## Brand

  - background: #E0EFFA;
  - primary: #006CBE;
  - primary-hover: #0078d4;
